Ductor Assistant

A personal AI assistant, run by one person on their own server.

Ductor is a private, single-user assistant. It runs on hardware owned by its operator and is used through a personal Telegram bot. It is not a product, it is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

What it does

  • Keeps notes, tasks and long-running projects in files on its own server.
  • Reads and writes the operator's own Google Calendar, Drive, Sheets and Tasks.
  • Uploads videos to the operator's own YouTube channel.
  • Runs scheduled jobs and reports what is happening on the server.

Who can use it

One person: the account owner. There are no other users, no shared access and no public interface.
